Career Navigator is independent and has no partnership with these organisations. Contact them directly; their own websites are the source of truth.

- **Refugee Talent Hub:** an employers' initiative that connects employers and refugee job seekers, with activities and the platform RefugeeWork. Activities need a free account.
- **VluchtelingenWerk Nederland (Dutch Council for Refugees):** supports refugees from arrival onwards, with volunteers, towards an independent life, including learning the language. It has local offices across the Netherlands.
- **UAF (Foundation for Refugee Students):** works with refugee students, professionals and scientists. It offers personal guidance and financial support for education from vocational to university, and help finding a place in the labour market.
- **UWV / werk.nl:** the public employment service. Its site werk.nl has free guides to CVs, letters, networking and interviews.
- **Your municipality (gemeente):** many municipalities run work programmes for people who receive social assistance or are in civic integration.
