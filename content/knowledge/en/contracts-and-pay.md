This card explains words you will meet in job offers. It is **not legal advice**. For your own situation, check the official pages below or ask a legal advice service.

**Temporary or permanent.** A contract can be **temporary** (bepaalde tijd, fixed-term) or **permanent** (onbepaalde tijd, indefinite). Temporary contracts cannot be extended forever: according to the government's business portal, an employee must get a permanent contract after **3 consecutive temporary contracts**, or after **3 years** of temporary contracts. A CAO can change this.

**Probation period (proeftijd).** A probation period counts only if it is agreed **in writing**. Its maximum length depends on the contract: none for contracts of 6 months or less, at most 1 month for temporary contracts of more than 6 months and less than 2 years, and at most 2 months for permanent contracts and temporary contracts of more than 2 years. During the probation period, both the employer and the employee can end the contract without a reason.

**CAO (collectieve arbeidsovereenkomst).** A collective labour agreement for a sector or company. It can set different rules, for example on pay or holiday allowance.

**Holiday allowance (vakantiegeld).** At least **8 euros for every 100 euros** of your gross annual salary. Most employers pay it once a year, in **May or June**. A CAO may contain different agreements.

**Example question to ask after an offer (fictional):** “Is this a temporary or a permanent contract, is there a probation period, and which CAO applies?”
