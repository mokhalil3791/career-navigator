- **Vacature:** a vacancy or job advert.
- **Sollicitatie / solliciteren:** a job application / to apply for a job.
- **Sollicitatiebrief / motivatiebrief:** application letter / motivation letter. See [werk.nl](https://www.werk.nl/nl/solliciteren/sollicitatiebrief).
- **Sollicitatiegesprek:** job interview. See [werk.nl](https://www.werk.nl/nl/solliciteren/sollicitatiegesprek).
- **Persoonlijk profiel / profielschets:** the short “about me” part at the top of a CV.
- **Werkervaring:** work experience.
- **Netwerken:** networking, making and keeping contact with people you know. See [werk.nl](https://www.werk.nl/nl/solliciteren/netwerken).
- **Koffiegesprek:** an informal “coffee conversation” to learn about a job or a field.
- **Open sollicitatie:** an application to an employer without a published vacancy.
- **Uitzendbureau:** a recruitment or temporary employment agency.
- **Werkervaringsplek:** a work-experience placement.
- **Traineeship:** a paid training programme for starters, often for one or two years.

- **Proeftijd:** probation period. It only counts if agreed in writing. See [Business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/trial-period/).
- **Bepaalde tijd / onbepaalde tijd:** temporary (fixed-term) / permanent contract. See [Business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/regulations/contract-employment/).
- **CAO (collectieve arbeidsovereenkomst):** a collective labour agreement for a sector or company.
- **Vakantiegeld:** holiday allowance: at least 8 euros for every 100 euros of gross annual salary. See [Business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/staff/terms-of-employment/paying-holiday-allowance-to-your-staff/).
- **VOG (Verklaring Omtrent het Gedrag):** certificate of conduct. See [Justis](https://www.justis.nl/en/products/certificate-of-conduct).
- **NT2 (Nederlands als tweede taal):** Dutch as a second language. See [Staatsexamens Nt2](https://www.staatsexamensnt2.nl/English).
- **Inburgering:** civic integration. See [inburgeren.nl](https://www.inburgeren.nl/en/).
- **Diplomawaardering:** credential evaluation of a foreign diploma (IDW). See [idw.nl](https://www.idw.nl/en/).
- **BSN (burgerservicenummer):** the personal public service number. Never put it on a CV, and never paste it into an AI chat.
