**Use more than one route.** Many people combine vacancy websites, their network and direct contact with employers.

**Networking (netwerken).** werk.nl describes networking as making and keeping contact with people you know. Your network can give tips about organisations that are looking for staff. You can network at work, at a sports club or at a birthday, and online through LinkedIn. werk.nl also notes that it works both ways: you help other people too.

**Changing profession?** werk.nl suggests finding someone who works in that profession, for example through LinkedIn, and asking what you need to be good at. Many people call this a **koffiegesprek** (a coffee conversation): a short, informal talk to learn, not a job interview.

**Example question for a koffiegesprek (fictional):** “What does a normal week look like in your team, and which skills helped you most in your first year?”

**Platforms for newcomers.** Refugee Talent Hub describes itself as an employers' initiative that connects employers and refugee job seekers, including through its platform RefugeeWork. It also runs activities; signing up needs a free account.

**Recruitment agencies (uitzendbureaus) and open applications (open sollicitatie)** are other common routes.
