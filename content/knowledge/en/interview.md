werk.nl describes a job interview as having a few usual parts:

1. **The start.** The interview already begins when you shake hands, often in the hall or the lift. You can start the small talk yourself.
2. **Introductions.** The interviewers introduce themselves, the organisation, the department and the work. You only need to listen with attention.
3. **“Tell me about yourself.”** Interviews often start like this. werk.nl suggests practising an **elevator pitch**: what you add, in about one minute.
4. **Questions both ways.** You answer the employer's questions, and you also ask your own. That helps you find out whether the job suits you, and it shows interest.
5. **The result.** Shortly after the last interview you usually hear whether you are hired. If you are, a conversation about salary and terms (arbeidsvoorwaardengesprek) usually follows.

werk.nl also mentions preparing your clothes, your questions and practical things such as checking the route.

**Example questions you could ask (fictional):**

- “What would success look like in the first three months?”
- “How does the team support someone who is still improving their Dutch?”

Interview styles vary by employer. Practise in **Interview practice** with the STAR structure (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
