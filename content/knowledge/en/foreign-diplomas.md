**What it is.** IDW compares your foreign study programme with the Dutch education system. The evaluation shows which **Dutch education level is comparable** to your diploma.

**What it is not.** IDW says the evaluation is advice: you cannot derive any legal rights from it. It is **usually not mandatory**, but an employer may ask for it.

**Which diplomas.** IDW evaluates nationally recognised diplomas for programmes of at least 1,000 hours. It does not evaluate short courses, work experience or Dutch diplomas. If you did not finish, an evaluation of education may be possible after at least one completed year.

**Cost.** IDW's page for work states that an evaluation costs **€148.83** (checked on 2026-10-23). Ask your municipality or a support organisation whether they can help with the cost.

**How to apply.** You apply online through **My IDW** on idw.nl. There are separate routes for study, work and civic integration (inburgering).

**On your CV (example, fictional):** “BSc Business Administration, Example University (Syria), 2018. IDW evaluation: comparable to a Dutch bachelor's degree (hbo).” Only write the comparison once you have the evaluation.

In **My profile**, keep the **original** title of your qualification. Do not assume a Dutch equivalence before you have an evaluation.
