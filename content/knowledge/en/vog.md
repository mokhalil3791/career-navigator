**What it is.** A VOG (Verklaring Omtrent het Gedrag) is a statement from the Dutch Minister of Legal Protection that you have not been convicted of a crime that is relevant to the job. Justis, the agency that issues it, checks the criminal records system and may also consult other sources.

**Why employers ask.** Justis says a VOG is often required for a new job. It is refused only when a conviction is relevant to the duties: for example, repeated drink-driving convictions for a taxi driver.

**Cost.** Justis lists a fee of **€41.35** (checked on 2026-10-23). Ask the employer whether they reimburse it.

**Who applies.** You apply yourself, usually after the employer has started the request. If you are not registered in the Dutch municipal records (BRP), you apply directly to Justis.

**In Career Navigator.** If an advert mentions a VOG, add it as a requirement with the status **Unclear** and a next step such as “Ask how the employer arranges the VOG.”
