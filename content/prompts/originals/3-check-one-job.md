I want to apply for this job: [job number from your list, or paste the job ad or send a screenshot]
1. Explain the job in simple English, in 3 sentences.
2. Give me a match score from 0 to 100, with a short reason.
3. List the requirements I clearly meet.
4. List the requirements I don't meet yet, and what I could do about each one.
5. List important keywords from the ad that are missing in my CV.
Be honest. Do not make the score higher to be nice.
