Search the internet for job vacancies in the Netherlands that match my CV (the CV in this chat, or the photo I send now).
I live in [your city] and can travel up to [for example 45 minutes]. My Dutch level is [for example A2]. I am looking for [full-time / part-time / any].
Search job websites such as werk.nl, Indeed, LinkedIn and Nationale Vacaturebank. Look for jobs that match my experience, and also for jobs one step lower that I could start with.
Give me the 5 best matches, ranked from best to worst. For each job:
- job title, company and city
- the link to the vacancy
- a match score from 0 to 100, with one sentence why
- whether Dutch is required, and at what level
Rules: only list jobs you really found in your search, with a real link. Do not invent jobs. If you find fewer than 5, say so.
At the end, ask me which job I want to work on.
