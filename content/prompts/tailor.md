Tailor an English CV draft to the selected advert, keeping employment history in reverse chronological order and bringing relevant evidence forward within each role. Use keywords only when supported. Keep dates, titles, proficiency and results accurate. Provide a separate change log showing each changed claim and its source. Mark missing evidence as a question, not a CV claim. If no advert is selected, ask for it.
