## Goal
Write my LinkedIn sections.

## Instruction
Write LinkedIn sections from my recorded facts only: a headline (at most 220 characters), an About section (at most 1,500 characters, first person, plain and friendly) and three experience bullets that each describe one real action and its factual result. Do not invent achievements, numbers, employers or credentials. Where a useful detail is missing, write a question in square brackets instead of a claim.
